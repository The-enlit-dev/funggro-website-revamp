# 02 — Brand & audience (what the site itself says; claims are NOT independently verified)

## What Funngro is
An earning app for young Indians. Users complete paid tasks for brands (surveys, sampling, referrals, content/influencer briefs, brand promotion), upload proof, and are paid by UPI. Legal entity in footer: **Wishbanc Technologies Private Limited** (brand: Funngro). The JSON-LD says "Funngro Innovations Pvt Ltd" — a mismatch (see audit).

## Positioning (verbatim site language)
- Tagline: "India's youth · India's brands. Get paid in UPI or bank transfer for the work you actually want to do."
- Home H1: "Get Paid by India's biggest brands with flexible remote opportunities."
- Tone: plain, confident, rupee-specific, Gen-Z-friendly ("Honest guides. Not hype.", "Real humans. Actual replies.").

## Audiences
1. **Primary — Teens/young earners, 14–25** (FAQ: "Any young Indian between 14 and 25"). Press/search results mention a >14 age floor with parental approval for teens; the website text I read does **not** state the parental-consent rule → **confirm with client** before the Teen page mentions age/consent.
   - Needs: a first income, flexibility, no investment, proof it isn't a scam, speed of payout.
   - Fears: scams, hidden fees, rejection of work, privacy.
2. **Secondary — Parents of teens.** The site hardly addresses them on the Teen page (only a legacy FAQ line about a "teen debit card"). Trust & safety content exists at `/trust` but is not linked from the Teen page body.
3. **Brands / advertisers** (For Brands page) — out of scope for the two pages but the About page is where brands judge credibility.
4. **Jobseekers / press / investors** — About page audience.
