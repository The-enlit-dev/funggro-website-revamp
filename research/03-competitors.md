## Patterns observed
- Competitors lead with an **earnings headline** and a **#1** claim. Funngro already differentiates with a rupee-specific ladder.
- Nobody in this set foregrounds **parent reassurance** or **age rules** — an opening for the Teen page (subject to client facts).
- Social proof formats that work here: logo walls, real-people stories, counts.
