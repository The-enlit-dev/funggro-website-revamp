About Funngro

Built so young Indians can earn for real.

Funngro connects young Indians aged 14–25 with paid work from real brands — so they can earn, learn real skills and get work experience early, using just a smartphone.

You shouldn’t have to wait until after college to earn your first income — or to do real work.

That’s the idea Funngro is built on. Instead of pocket money alone, young people get real projects from real brands: surveys, product sampling, referrals, content and promotion — each with a clear brief and a clear payout.

Its earliest press coverage focused on exactly this: real-world work experience and money skills for teenagers. Today the app is used across India, from big cities to small towns.

The Teenlancer engine

A company run by the people who use it.

Funngro calls its young earners Teenlancers. Much of the work of running the company is done by people who started out as users.

  • Support & onboarding

    Answering user questions and helping brands set up campaigns.

  • Quality assurance

    Community-powered testing — releases are checked by the people who use them.

  • Content & design

    Social, in-app and marketing creatives, made by the audience itself.

  • Community & clans

    Clan Leaders guide new earners, share projects and answer questions.

How we work

Rules we hold ourselves to.

Read Trust & safety
  1. 01

    Every campaign is reviewed

    Briefs pass content moderation and brand verification before they go live. No adult content, gambling or MLM.

  2. 02

    Free for young people

    No joining fee and no subscription. Brands pay Funngro, not the people doing the work.

  3. 03

    Data stays in India

    Minimal data, stored in India. Users can delete their account from inside the app.
