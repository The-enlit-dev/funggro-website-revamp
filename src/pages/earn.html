How it works

From sign-up to your first withdrawal, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Download the app and sign up

    Get Funngro from Google Play or the App Store. Sign-up takes about two minutes with OTP verification — no investment, no joining fee.

  2. 2

    Pick a brand campaign

    Browse live campaigns — brand promotion, sampling, surveys, referrals and content. Each one shows what to do and what it pays.

  3. 3

    Do the task and upload proof

    Follow the brief, then submit a photo, screenshot or link. The work is checked against the brand’s instructions — if something’s missing, you’ll get feedback to fix it.

  4. 4

    Get approved, then withdraw

    Approved earnings land in your Funngro wallet. Withdraw to UPI or your bank account from the Wallet tab. Review time depends on the campaign — each project shows its own timeline.

Questions

What teens (and parents) ask us.

Who can join Funngro?

Funngro is for young Indians aged 14 to 25. Signing up takes about two minutes with OTP verification.

Is it free?

Yes. There’s no joining fee and no subscription. Funngro is paid by brands, not by you.

How do I get paid?

Approved earnings are added to your Funngro wallet. Open Wallet, tap Withdraw, add your UPI or bank details and confirm. Major UPI apps such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and BHIM are supported.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the campaign. Some tasks are checked quickly; others, like content, need a manual review. Each project’s description shows its timeline.

What kinds of tasks can I do?

Four main kinds: brand promotion, referrals, micro tasks (sampling, surveys, app testing, ideation) and content creation (reviews, short videos and other user-generated content). New campaigns are added regularly.

Can I earn without a PAN card?

Yes. You can earn without a PAN card through simple quick tasks (Funngro calls them Toffee Projects — surveys, app downloads and games), through Share & Earn, and through contests.

What can get an account suspended?

Fake referrals, fake submissions, spamming, misusing campaigns or breaking the platform’s guidelines can lead to suspension.
