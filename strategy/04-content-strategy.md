# 04 — Content strategy

## Principles
1. **Only sourced facts.** Every number on the page maps to a row in `research/06-verified-facts.md`. Tier C → visible placeholder until confirmed. Tier D → never.
2. **Label money.** Every rupee figure says what it is ("typical range", "average active user, FY26, reported by Funngro").
3. **Date volatile numbers.** Store rating/downloads shown as "on Google Play, Nov 2026" and refreshed at launch.
4. **Write for a 15-year-old, check with a parent.** Short sentences (≤20 words), Indian English, ₹ and lakh, no jargon unless explained.
5. **One idea per section, one CTA per screen.**
6. **No hype words:** no "#1", "guaranteed", "instant money", "passive income", "get rich", "limited time".

## Voice
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Specific ("₹200–1,500 per brand-promotion task") | Vague ("earn big") |
| Honest ("pay depends on the campaign; some tasks need review") | Overpromising ("instant cash") |
| Warm, peer-to-peer | Corporate or preachy |
| Confident | Shouty |

## Handling the contradictions (proposed wording strategy — needs client OK)
| Topic | Proposed copy approach |
|---|---|
| Payout speed | "Withdraw to UPI or bank once your task is approved. Review time depends on the campaign." (avoids both "seconds" and "10 days" until the client picks) |
| Fees | "Free to join. No subscription." — omit "zero withdrawal fees" until confirmed |
| Users | Use **50L+ downloads on Google Play** (verifiable) instead of 70L/60L users |
| Founding year / founder titles | Placeholder until confirmed |
| Age | "For ages 14–25" (matches site + store text) + `{{parent consent wording}}`; flag App Store 16+ |
