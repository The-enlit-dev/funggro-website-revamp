## Content removed vs today (and why)
- The "6 pillars" and "7-step progression" — repeat steps and the ladder.
- SEO-stuffed phrases ("online work for earning", "best refer-and-earn app") — read as spam to the audience most afraid of scams.
- Unexplained jargon (GroMoney, Groscore) — mentioned only where explained.
